# Cross-Domain Architecture (Standalone Bridge Document)

**Author:** Ryan W. Yett (`Mega-Therion`) | **ORCID:** [0009-0001-1303-7190](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-1303-7190)

This document is the **single** cross-domain synthesis bridge in the `Mega-Therion` ecosystem. Individual track repositories are intentionally isolated so each discipline can be evaluated on its own domain-native standards.

## Shared Methodological Spine

1. **Epistemic Fencing (`[P]/[D]/[E]/[O]/[X]`):** Every claim is tagged by verification status before publication.
2. **Limits-First Reporting:** Negative results, open conjectures (`[O]`), and irreducible parameter counts are stated in the opening paragraph of every repository and paper.
3. **Deterministic Verification:** Lean 4 (`0`-sorry kernels where `[P]`), SymPy symbolic checks, and reproducible benchmark scripts (`pytest`, `cargo test`, `lake build`).
4. **Domain Isolation:** Physics (`Res-Nova`), formal math (`4Leibniz`), AI runtime (`S.E.L.I.N`), semiotics (`RYTT`), and civic policy (`Arkansas`) do not cross-link inside spoke headers.
